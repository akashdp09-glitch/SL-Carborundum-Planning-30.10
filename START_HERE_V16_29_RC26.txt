SL CARBORUNDUM PLANNING CENTER — V16.29-RC26 (HISTORICAL)

QUICK START (WINDOWS PC)
1. Extract this ZIP to a NEW folder. Keep RC25 and existing data folders until RC26 is checked.
2. Connect the PC to company Wi-Fi and sign in to Windows with the account that has the Z: drive mapped.
3. Double-click START_SL_CARBORUNDUM_PLANNING_CENTER.bat. If Z: is unavailable or read-only, startup stops and explains the issue. It does not save data to D: as a fallback.
4. Leave the black server window running. Open the Planning Center on the PC. Use the displayed factory-LAN address on phones. The phone sends scans/photos to the app; the PC app writes them to Z:.
5. For live QR camera scanning, use the app's HTTPS camera link and the certificate instructions. HTTP supports ordinary web access and photo upload; a real phone camera test is still required.

AUDIT PHOTO RECOVERY
On startup, RC26 checks the old D:\SL_Carborundum_Planning_V16.29-rc14\CENTRAL_DATA folders for photo files referenced by the Z: database and copies missing photos into the new Z: photo folder. It also merges unmatched audit rows from the old D: database additively. Both old database and photo folders are left untouched. Confirm audit counts and open several historical photos before removing any old folder.

RECOVERY PLAN + KPIs
In Weekly Report, download “Weekly + Recovery Action Pack”. Its Recovery Actions sheet lists urgent open orders and leaves Sales priority/date and Production capacity/feasible finish for cross-functional confirmation. The customer due date is not changed by the software.
The Monthly Planning KPI page retains its controlled monthly measures and adds employee-specific KPI definitions and evidence for weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom periods. KPI results require a source/evidence reference.
