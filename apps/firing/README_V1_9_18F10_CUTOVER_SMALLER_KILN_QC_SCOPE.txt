F10 CUTOVER / POSITIVE PPC RULES
1. User-selected First Firing Date is the global new-plan cutover. Dates before it follow previous issued plan/actual execution.
2. Previous issued firing files may be uploaded; prior kiln cycles are protected into the new horizon.
3. New plan starts from cutover, due date first. Missing upstream quantity is a production commitment only when shared Preparation/Mixing/Forming capacity can supply it before firing.
4. If a large kiln is under-filled, test smaller compatible kiln before delaying due date.
5. Optimize physical fit, height, volume and program-weight utilization after due-date protection.
6. Suggested Safe Revised Due Date is only after feasible kiln alternatives/capacity timing are exhausted.
7. QC is not a PPC production section; QC duration is considered in downstream timing by Daily Planner.
