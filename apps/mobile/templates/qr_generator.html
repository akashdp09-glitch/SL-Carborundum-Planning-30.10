Print labels: selected work orders and temporary IDs

Each label shows the human-readable ID, part information and a QR. Temporary-ID labels are marked "TEMPORARY - NOT A WORK ORDER". Enter one or more IDs (work order IDs or UNID-… temporary IDs), separated by spaces, commas or new lines.

The labels carry a stable identity (SLWO: / SLTMP:), not a network address, so they keep working if the PC address changes. A phone's built-in camera app cannot open an identity QR; use Scan in this app. Use the direct-open QR above only when a stable HTTPS address is configured.
