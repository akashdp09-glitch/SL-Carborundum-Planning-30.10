NEW: Sidebar > Planning Suite > Generate bond / material plans.
1. Seed open/not-mixed requirements from active Plan-2026, or enter demand from
   a project, RP/WO or forecast. Seed quantities are FULL WO requirements, not
   automatically remaining unconsumed demand. Verify partial mixing usage.
2. Confirm NEED DATE and kg. A due-date fallback is explicitly labelled and
   must be replaced by the actual material need date. Review all recipe quantities.
3. Inventory: available/released stock only, safety, calendar lead days, lot size,
   minimum order. A blank required field blocks the code. 0 lot means unrestricted
   ordering only when approved. Stock seed requires your current-stock verification.
4. Enter confirmed incoming receipts with dates. LAB/held/unconfirmed/overdue
   receipts are not silently made available. Same-day confirmed receipts are
   assumed available before demand; verify this operational convention.
5. Optional BOND recipe: approved component kg per batch and output kg per batch,
   with source reference. Generates component demand with rounded batch count.
   This is not a chemistry designer. Capacity/lead time for bond preparation needs
   confirmation; purchase lead time must not be mistaken for production runtime.
6. Save snapshot and calculate. Restore scenarios from the dropdown after navigation.
7. Download proposals, dated ledger, diagnostics, input snapshots, recipe components,
   weekly/monthly/quarterly/half-year/yearly requirements. Proposed receipts are
   hypothetical and shown separately from confirmed supply.

Storage: <SL_SHARED_ROOT>/DATA/planning_scenarios.sqlite (actual path on screen).
