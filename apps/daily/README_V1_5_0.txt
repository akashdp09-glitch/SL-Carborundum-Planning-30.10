SL PPC DAILY PLANNER V1.5.0

Confirmed changes:
1. Grinding and Cutting are presented as one common post-firing route so Plan 2026 combined post-fire WIP is never hidden by route inference.
2. Capacity vs Plan vs Actual shows tons, pcs, planned/actual unique Work Orders. Equivalent pcs capacity is explicitly labelled as product-mix equivalent.
3. WIP is shown by process AND product category P/R/S/T/M/PB, with fixed category colors, pcs, tons and unique WO counts.
4. Long-term MPS adds Sales Forecast category/group and size-range views.
5. MRP uses distinct material colors and retains WHAT / WHEN / HOW MUCH purchase actions.
6. Stock-on-hand material graph added.
7. TV Dashboard added: urgent/delivery-risk WOs, current status/bottleneck, WIP by process/category, TODAY/TOMORROW targets with customer, WO and quantity.
8. Dashboard can be downloaded as large-screen JPEG.
9. Existing start-date lock and previous-issued-firing bridge are retained.

Important: dashboard and calculations use the Plan 2026, Firing Plan, Capacity DB, Stock and Sales Forecast uploaded at runtime. The program does not hard-code a historical Plan 2026 snapshot.
