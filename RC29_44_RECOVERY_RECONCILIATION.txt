V16.29-rc29.44 — Recovery and Current Plan Reconciliation

Plan-2026 is the ongoing source for current work-order state. Each Production recovery workbook is a dated snapshot of recovery activity and can contain older commitments from several months.

Reports → Recovery Snapshot & Product Master Review now shows a work-order bridge between those sources. It compares:
- historical due date in the Recovery workbook;
- Production proposed recovery date or hold text and source result;
- current Plan-2026 due date, open quantity, whole-order closure and section/location;
- source sheet row references for both files;
- whether the WO is still overdue under the shared current overdue rule.

The analysis distinguishes a still-overdue order from a historical Recovery order no longer overdue today. It explains if the current Plan says closed, has no positive balance, has a later due date (authorization must be checked), has no usable date, or has no matching WO. Current Plan overdue WOs absent from the dated Recovery workbook are shown separately. It does not treat a Recovery entry as Production approval, overwrite the old date, or erase historical lateness. CSV exports are available for both lists.

Product registration and Production packaging comparison from RC29.43 remains available on the same page.
