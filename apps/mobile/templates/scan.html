How this works

  1. Set this PC as a station once, e.g. /scan?station=GR-01.
  2. Operator scans the Work Order QR.
  3. The software identifies the job, product, quantity and current status automatically.
  4. Operator taps one large action.
  5. The database records time, user, terminal, process and location.
No typing of customer name or specification is required.
