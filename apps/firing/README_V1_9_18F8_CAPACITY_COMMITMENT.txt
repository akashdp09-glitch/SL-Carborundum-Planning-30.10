F8 POSITIVE CAPACITY COMMITMENT
- Kiln schedule shows ALL committed planned loads, not only RELEASE-ready loads.
- If missing upstream quantity is within planned capacity/lead-time, it is treated as achievable production and the kiln stays in the plan.
- Ready pcs + MAKE pcs = planned firing qty.
- Due date remains first priority; then compatible combination; volume/height/program-weight utilization; then daily workload balance.
- RELEASE remains a shop-floor gate and does not hide future planned kilns.
- Hard physical/capacity impossibility remains an exception.
