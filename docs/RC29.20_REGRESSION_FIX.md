# RC29.20 — WIP and backorder consistency

**Date:** 9 October 2026 (Phan Thong, Chonburi)  
**Status:** Release candidate; synthetic checks passed. Live Plan-2026 and section-WIP files were not available for acceptance testing.

## What caused the views to disagree

- Home and Daily Planner independently mapped due dates. Home was replacing the planner's revised delivery date (Plan-2026 column E) with the original warehouse date (column C).
- Long-range MPS used a separate open-balance calculation and split firm orders relative to the selected MPS month. An overdue order due earlier in the current month could therefore appear in both firm demand and backorder recovery.
- MPS grouped future firm orders by month number without checking calendar year, so a 2027 order could be included in a 2026 monthly horizon.
- The Home summary's WIP graph showed Plan-2026 open order balance only; it omitted the Production section WIP files that provide the physical WIP snapshot.

## Rules now used

- **As-of date:** current local date in Phan Thong, Chonburi.
- **Due date:** revised delivery date, column E; initial date, column C, is used only when E is blank.
- **Open balance:** Plan-2026 QTY minus Packing Actual, floored at zero.
- **Whole-WO closure:** Packing Status (EU) or final production closure (FC). Section completion by itself does not close the whole WO.
- **Backorder:** open positive balance with due date strictly before the as-of date. Due today, future-due and missing-date orders are not overdue.
- **MPS demand buckets:** overdue recovery and future firm demand are disjoint. Future firm demand must fall within the selected calendar year and MPS month horizon. Missing due dates remain visible for review rather than being assigned to an invented month.
- **WIP:** the Home dashboard now shows the Production-reported section/category stacked gross-tonne graph with source filenames and recorded import times. Planning WIP remains separately labelled and is not added to Production WIP.

## Where to compare

| Page | Backorder view | WIP view |
|---|---|---|
| Home dashboard | Past-due quantity by revised due month and category; totals derived from the same chart aggregation | Production-reported stacked gross tonnes by section and category, plus the existing Plan-2026 planning balance |
| Daily Planner → Backorder Analysis | WO-level strict overdue list and monthly/category totals | Daily Planner/Reports retains separate Plan-2026 planning WIP and Production snapshot WIP measures |
| Daily Planner → MPS / Recovery | Overdue WOs feed recovery once; future firm WOs feed only the selected year's demand | MPS capacity is a horizon feasibility comparison, not a physical WIP total |

For equal Plan-2026 source revision, cut-off date and display filters, Home and Daily Planner's **strict overdue** WO and quantity totals should agree. The broader delivery-risk/recovery list can be larger because it may include due-soon orders, risk orders, and missing-date checks. Production WIP totals will differ from planning WIP because their source and weight basis differ; they must not be added together.
