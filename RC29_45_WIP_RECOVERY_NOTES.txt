V16.29-rc29.45 — Production WIP and Recovery / Plan Separation
Build basis: full V16.29-rc29.44 package, with traceable section WIP and Production Recovery reconciliation updates.

DEFINITIONS
- Production WIP is read from each section's monthly transaction workbook. Open WIP work-order count is the summary's Total WO minus Closed WO, cross-checked against open detail rows. Open WIP tonnes use the detail rows' Total GW (Kg) / 1,000. Closed work orders are not subtracted from weight; the summary's GW (Kg) is the gross weight of open WIP.
- Plan-2026 WIP/order balance is a separate demand/production-planning measure from the active Plan-2026 workbook. It is never added to or substituted for Production's physical section WIP.
- Recovery status is a dated Production snapshot, not the same thing as strict overdue status in Plan-2026. The app reports source status and reconciles each WO to the active Plan separately.
- F category maps to P as owner-approved. Missing/#N/A category values are preserved as SOURCE CATEGORY MISSING; no P/PB/R category is invented.

WHAT TO DO IN THE APP
Upload each current section WIP workbook into the Production WIP input; upload the latest Plan-2026 and latest Production Recovery snapshot in their source panels. The pages use the shared active copies. Use the WIP source checks and the Recovery vs Plan report to inspect each workbook/sheet/row reference. Do not replace or delete CENTRAL_DATA when updating the program.
