# V16.29-rc29.24 — QR filters and A4 cut-ready labels

The QR label page now has three status choices: Open orders (positive open balance only), Closed orders (Plan-2026 authoritative closure flag), and All orders. Users can filter by current due date or work-order creation date, choose a from/to range, and optionally include orders whose selected date is missing. Filters use the active Plan-2026; no stale mobile database rows are used.

The tracking sheet records order quantity, remaining quantity, status, creation date, due date, section/location and source row. QR payload remains `SLWO:<UPPERCASE WO>`. On the `Sticker Print` sheet, each label prints its uppercase WO beneath its QR. The print area is A4 portrait, 3 columns by 4 rows per page (12 labels), with dashed cut guides for scissors. Confirm print scaling is 100% / fit-to-one-page-wide and check one paper sheet before applying stickers.
