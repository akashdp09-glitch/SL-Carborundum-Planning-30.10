SL PPC DAILY PLANNER V1.7.2 - SAFE SALES DUE-DATE RECOMMENDATION
10-Sep-2026

Added:
- Suggested Safe Due Date per open WO.
- Recommendation uses scheduled Packing completion + configurable working-day protection buffer (default 2 working days).
- Full schedule coverage is required before a date is recommended.
- If remaining quantity cannot be fully scheduled because of capacity/resource/route issues, result is DATE NOT SAFE TO COMMIT rather than an invented date.
- Delivery Risk tab shows Suggested Safe Due Date, extension, action, confidence, schedule coverage, packing completion and basis.
- Excel output contains a dedicated 'Sales Due Date Request' sheet.
- Existing configurable Plan 2026 mapping remains unchanged, including Mixing Status = DH and REJECT = FH.
