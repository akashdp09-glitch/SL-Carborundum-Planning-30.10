V1.8.4 — WEEKLY MEETING REPORT

Added new DAILY PLANNER tab: WEEKLY REPORT.

Management summary:
- User-selected FROM / TO planning window.
- Incoming open WOs by product group.
- Open due demand.
- Planned WOs / qty / ton.
- Department demand vs available capacity.
- Bottleneck department and load % where comparable capacity is available.
- Delivery-risk counts.
- Production mitigation recommendation.

Detailed Gantt:
- Selects WOs whose calculated work starts/plans within FROM–TO.
- Shows the COMPLETE calculated production route for those WOs, including processes after TO.
- Product Group / Category uses a fixed color map.
- The same category colors are used in the product-group summary chart and detailed Gantt.
- No raw Plotly duration numbers are printed on timeline bars.

Weekly Excel download:
Weekly Summary / Dept Capacity / WO Complete Gantt / Delivery Risk / Window Plan Detail.
