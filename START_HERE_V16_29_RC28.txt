SL CARBORUNDUM PLANNING CENTER — V16.29-RC28 (HISTORICAL)

QUICK START (WINDOWS APP PC)
1. Close the old app. Keep the existing data folder; do not replace or delete it.
2. Extract this ZIP into a new program folder on D:, for example D:\SL_Carborundum_Planning_V16.29-rc28.
3. Double-click START_SL_CARBORUNDUM_PLANNING_CENTER.bat. This version uses local D: storage and does not require Z: or a mapped network drive.
4. Leave the server window open. Open the Planning Center on this PC. Operators connect to the PC app over factory Wi-Fi; phones do not read/write D: directly.
5. For phone live-camera QR scanning, use the app's HTTPS camera link and follow docs\HTTPS.md. Confirm camera scanning on the actual phone before production use.

DATA SAFETY
Before updating, stop the app and copy the complete CENTRAL_DATA folder to a dated backup. Unzip code to a new folder; never unzip over CENTRAL_DATA. Keep your previous app folder until the new version's database, audit counts, photos and report exports are checked. The package is a candidate and still needs a trial on the factory PC and a real phone camera.
