Business distinction:
Sales' registered master records registered product identity/code, customer-facing description/brand, and sales pack fields. Production's standard records the shop-floor packaging recipe: pack quantity and packing-material codes/quantities. These are related but have different owners and purposes. Exact code and pack quantity comparisons surface the difference; only the responsible owner should approve a correction.
