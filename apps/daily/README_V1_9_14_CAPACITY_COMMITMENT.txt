V1.9.14 POSITIVE CAPACITY COMMITMENT
- Feasible process demand is treated as an achievable production commitment, not a reason to suppress downstream plans.
- Risk/bottleneck views keep WO, customer, category, qty, due date/month, process, required date and complete process plan.
- Under-capacity sections are instructed to achieve planned qty and use remaining capacity for next-earliest executable demand.
- Only quantified overload/timing/hard-constraint portions are escalated.
- Daily Status errors remain isolated in the Daily Status tab.
